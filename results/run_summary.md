## What changed

- **Part 1:** datasets regenerated with the junction-area netlists. KCL, sanity and trend checks
  all pass (`results/part1/validation_summary.txt`).
- **Part 2: PMOS ON now uses LightGBM, not the MLP.** The selection score is the mean of
  (median + p95)/2 over terminals. On validation, the MLP had the lower median (2.45% vs 5.38%) but a
  higher p95 (35.3% vs 29.8%), so LightGBM won. NMOS ON, NMOS OFF and PMOS OFF still use the MLP.
  The PMOS ON uniform-test error rose from 1.9% to 3.8% median, which accounts for most of the
  transistor-level change.
- **Part 2, OFF-device Ib:** with junction areas, most OFF-transistor body currents now fall below
  the 1 fA scoring floor (`I_FLOOR`). NMOS OFF uniform Ib is scored on 113 of 750 rows (749 before),
  so its error is computed on far fewer rows, and the remaining ones are harder.
- **Part 3:** gate SPICE records (`spice_gates.csv`) and the oracle check are byte-identical to
  before; only the ML predictions changed. NOR2 `10` / `11` remain the hardest cases (3.9% / 3.3%
  median).
- **Part 4:** circuit SPICE results are identical to before (checkpoints reused by netlist hash); only
  the ML-side tables, plots and report were regenerated.
